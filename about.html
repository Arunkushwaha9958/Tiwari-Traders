Our Story

From a trading counter to a graded-media supply chain.

The journey of Tiwari Traders began in 1990 with a vision to deliver high-quality water filtration solutions across India. From our corporate office in Barakar, West Bengal, we started our operations by supplying essential filtration materials such as Filtering Sand, Filtering Gravels, Filtering Quartz Silica, MnO₂, Activated Carbon, and Anthracite.
Over the years, our unwavering commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction has helped us grow into a trusted name in the industry. Today, Tiwari Traders is recognized as one of the leading manufacturers of All Types of Water Filter Media Materials, serving customers with products that meet the highest industry standards.

The foundation of our success has been built under the leadership of our Director, Mr. Shiw Shankar Tiwari, who has dedicated more than 32 years to the water filtration industry. His commitment to excellence, integrity, and customer-first service has earned the trust and appreciation of clients across the country.
