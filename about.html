Our Story

From a trading counter to a graded-media supply chain.

Tiwari Traders started as a regional supplier of construction aggregates and gradually specialised in water filtration media as demand from treatment plants and RO system integrators grew. Today our focus is narrow and deliberate: four product lines — filtering gravel, filtering sand, quartz silica sand and quartz gravels — each screened into tightly controlled size grades.

We work directly with quarry and washing partners, then re-screen, wash and moisture-check every batch ourselves before it is packed. That extra step is what lets us commit to a size grade on paper and hold it in the bag.

What We Hold Ourselves To

Four commitments behind every dispatch.

01

Sieve-Verified Grading

Every size grade is checked against IS/ASTM sieve sets before packing, not just at the quarry.

02

Low Fines & Attrition

Washed media with minimal dust and fines content, reducing early filter clogging and backwash frequency.

03

Consistent Batches

The bag you reorder six months later performs the same as the one you tested first.

04

Direct Communication

One point of contact from enquiry to dispatch — no relay through multiple agents.
