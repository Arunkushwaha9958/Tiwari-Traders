In Detail

What each service actually includes.

Tiwari Traders is dedicated to offering customized supply chain solutions. We assist our customers in selecting high-quality and suitable products according to their specific requirements. As a trusted manufacturer and supplier, Tiwari Traders is committed to delivering industry-leading products backed by reliable manufacturing capabilities and strong research and development expertise. We ensure the highest standards of quality, dependable products, and comprehensive solutions tailored to meet the unique needs of every customer.

  • Custom size grades outside our standard catalogue, on request
  • Custom bag sizes: 25 kg, 50 kg, jumbo bags or bulk loose
  • Blended media where a single layer needs two grain sizes

Tiwari Traders maintains strict supervision over every stage of its operations—from sourcing high-quality raw materials and managing the production process to ensuring product quality and timely delivery. Every step is carefully monitored to ensure complete compliance with our customers' requirements. To uphold the highest quality standards, Tiwari Traders follows a rigorous two-level quality control process. In addition to the quality inspections carried out at our manufacturing plants, we perform comprehensive and thorough pre-shipment inspections to ensure that every product delivered meets our strict quality standards and customer expectations.

  • Sieve analysis against IS/ASTM standards for effective size and uniformity coefficient
  • Moisture content and acid-solubility checks for quartz-based media
  • Fines and dust content kept within agreed tolerance
  • Batch test reports shared with every dispatch on request

According to our customers' requirements, Tiwari Traders offers flexible transportation and logistics solutions, including road, rail, sea, and air freight, as well as LCL, FCL, and bulk cargo shipments. We also provide value-added services such as shipment tracking, customs support, inventory management, and efficient logistics coordination to ensure timely and reliable deliveries.

  • Road and rail dispatch across India, bulk or bagged
  • Bulker-truck loading for large volume filter media orders
  • Dispatch tracking and delivery scheduling coordination
  • Export packing and documentation support for overseas orders

Tiwari Traders provides one-to-one professional sales service. Besides a dedicated offline customer service team, Tiwari Traders has established a comprehensive and efficient sales service system that includes order production tracking, logistics support, technical guidance on product usage, business consultation, and customer satisfaction surveys.

  • Bed-depth and layer-sequence recommendations for new filters
  • Support for re-grading and re-media of existing filter vessels
  • Troubleshooting for filters showing early breakthrough or high head-loss

Tiwari Traders provides timely information on relevant industry trends, including supply updates, new products and applications, logistics, market insights, and policy-related information, helping our customers stay informed and make well-informed business decisions.

  • Product datasheets with size grade, bulk density and mineral composition
  • Sieve-analysis and test certificates per batch
  • Packaging, storage and handling guidance
